# Find the White Rabbit

The shared language for one guided photography exhibition.

## Language

**Exhibition**:

The single online digital photography exhibition, rather than a general-purpose portfolio or image library.

**Artwork**:

One publicly approved photograph in the curated exhibition sequence. It has a stable media key and its visitor-facing descriptive metadata.

**Curated sequence**:

The intentional emotional ordering of the exhibition's 14 MVP artworks. It is not a chronological feed. The final artwork is a visible concluding work before the ending message.

**Final artwork**:

The visible concluding photograph. It follows the same first-pass timing as every other artwork. Following its White Rabbit leads to the framed END and unlocks the Dock.

**White Rabbit**:

The exhibition's primary narrative invitation to move forward. It is the visual next-work cue in every system motion setting; it must never obscure protected viewing or navigation areas.

**White Rabbit mode**:

The first-pass exhibition mode. The visitor advances only by following the visible White Rabbit; it has no persistent next-work control and no direct previous/next keyboard navigation.

**Direct navigation**:

The Dock route to a photograph, available only after reaching END. There is no Home, About, Next, or Restart control. Revisiting a loaded photo reveals its rabbit immediately.

**Dock**:

The visitor-opened circular photograph gallery, entered from a softly glowing, floating circle after completing the first pass. It is available on END, revisited photographs, and the introduction after a same-tab refresh. It contains photos only.

**Introduction**:

The framed exhibition title and existing description with a Start button. No photograph timers run before Start. It replaces the old navigation and About modal.

**END**:

A blank exhibition frame with handwritten END. Arriving here marks completion and unlocks the Dock. No rabbit or restart control appears here.

**Completion**:

The visitor has followed the final photograph's rabbit into END. It grants free revisiting for the current tab session, including refresh, rather than permanently unlocking future independent visits.

**Paused rabbit session**:

The rabbit is temporarily hidden while the visitor is browsing the Dock. The Dock is available only after completion; closing it restores the same rabbit position without a forced wait.

**Motion settings**:

There is one rabbit-led route. System reduced-motion preference does not hide the rabbit or replace it with a Next control. This explicit 2026-09-19 decision replaces the earlier reduced-motion product branch.

**Exhibition media**:

Public, web-ready JPEG derivatives delivered to visitors for the first release. They are distinct from the private source originals, which are never published in the application repository or public storage.

**Artwork ownership**:

The 14 MVP artworks are original photographs owned by the exhibition creator.

**Public artwork metadata**:

The title, sequence label, bilingual exhibition text, and accurate alt text shown to visitors. Photographer name, location, year, and equipment information are excluded from the first release.

**Bilingual presentation**:

Short Chinese and English exhibition text shown together in clearly separated sections on the same page. It does not require language-specific routes or a language switcher in the first release.

**Content risk review**:

The manual, artwork-by-artwork review before public release for identifiable, sensitive, or otherwise concerning people depicted in a photograph. A concerning artwork is excluded from the launch sequence or made non-identifying before release.

**Link-only preview**:

The pre-release exhibition available to people who possess its URL but not intentionally listed or promoted publicly. It is used for visual and content approval before the formal domain is connected.
